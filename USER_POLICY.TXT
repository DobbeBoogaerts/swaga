# SWAGA
## User Policy
*Connecting the world. Respecting everyone.*

Welcome to **SWAGA**, a world-renowned social media platform built to connect people, inspire creativity, and bring communities together. By using SWAGA, you agree to the following principles.

### 1. Respect Everyone
Treat all users with dignity. Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, and discrimination are not permitted.

### 2. Be Authentic
Share genuine content and represent yourself honestly. Impersonation, fraudulent accounts, and deceptive activity are prohibited.

### 3. Share Responsibly
You are responsible for the content you publish. Do not share illegal material, threats, private information without consent, or content that exploits others.

### 4. Protect Privacy
Respect the privacy of others. Never expose personal information, access accounts without permission, or misuse another person's data.

### 5. Keep SWAGA Safe
Spam, scams, malicious links, manipulation of engagement, and attempts to compromise the platform are prohibited.

### 6. Own Your Content
Respect intellectual property. Only publish content you own or have permission to use. You retain ownership of your content, subject to the rights you grant SWAGA through its applicable terms.

### 7. Fair and Transparent Moderation
SWAGA may review reported content and take proportionate action against policy violations, including content removal, account restrictions, or suspension. Users should have access to an appropriate appeal process.

### 8. Protect the Community
SWAGA is committed to a safe, inclusive, and responsible digital environment. We aim to protect users while respecting freedom of expression and applicable laws.

### 9. Your Data, Your Rights
SWAGA will explain how personal information is collected, used, stored, and shared in its Privacy Policy. Users may exercise their applicable privacy rights in accordance with relevant law.

### 10. Our Shared Responsibility
By using SWAGA, you agree to follow this policy and any applicable platform terms. Serious or repeated violations may result in account suspension or termination.
